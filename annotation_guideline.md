# SARcasmScope-Civic annotation guideline

Each annotator labels every comment alone. Read the **comment to judge** together with its video title and, for replies, the parent comment.

## Labels
- **Yes (sarcastic):** the writer means something different from (usually the opposite of) what the words literally say, in order to mock, complain or joke.
  *Test:* would the writer agree with the literal meaning of their own words? If not, label Yes.
  Typical cases: mock praise of a bad situation, exaggerated agreement used to ridicule, ironic thanks.
- **No (not sarcastic):** literal praise, literal complaints (even angry ones), plain jokes without irony, questions, information.
- **Blank:** unreadable, wrong language, or impossible to judge. Blank cells are excluded from the vote count.

## Rules
1. Label alone; hide the other annotators' columns and do not discuss items until everyone has finished.
2. Do not use ChatGPT or any other AI tool.
3. An emoji alone does not make a comment sarcastic; a laughing emoji is often literal.
4. Judge whether the comment is meant literally, not whether you agree with the opinion.

## Final label
The majority of the three votes. Comments with fewer than two valid votes are excluded.
